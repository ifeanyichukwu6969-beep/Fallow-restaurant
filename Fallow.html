Our Philosophy

Root-to-Tip.
Nose-to-Tail.

Fallow was born from a radical idea: use everything. Our kitchen wastes nothing — every ingredient is sourced with intention and treated with deep respect, from whole animals to foraged herbs.

Set in the heart of Haymarket, our contemporary dining room is where sustainability meets exceptional craft. Join us at the Chef's Counter for an immersive kitchen experience, or settle in for a long, indulgent lunch.
